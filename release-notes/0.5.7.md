## 下载与任务管理

- 点击列表底部的下载中、已完成或失败等状态统计，可直接筛选对应任务；鼠标悬停与选中状态更清楚。
- 解析与下载分开排队，各支持最多 3 项并发。下载名额占满时，后续任务仍可提前解析标题、封面和格式；直播继续使用独立名额。
- 暂停或重启后复用已保存的解析结果和匹配的下载分片。媒体地址被拒绝时最多重新解析一次，不同来源的分片保持隔离。
- 媒体已下载但字幕等附件失败时，重试可继续处理本地来源，减少重复下载。

## 稳定性与处理改进

- 修复异常退出后临时 Cookie 和解析信息文件残留；下次启动取得数据目录锁后安全清理，保留已保存的登录信息。
- 修复删除未完成任务但保留媒体时，分片和处理中间文件失去管理记录的问题。保留已导出文件，并保护位于旧缓存目录中的成品。
- 满足条件的 SDR H.264/MKV 封面片头可复用主体视频流，减少完整重编码，继续验证成品与附件时间轴。
- 更新 Vitest 测试依赖以修复已知漏洞，并修正重名保护验收对提前解析文件的判断。

## 升级说明

- 升级前请完全退出应用（包括托盘进程）或停止旧容器，并保留原数据与下载目录。
- 旧任务没有解析快照，或来源地址已变化时，恢复仍可能重新下载；不会将不同来源的分片强行拼接。
- 本次不会自动扫描或删除旧版本已经留下、且没有任务记录的孤立缓存。
- macOS 仍要求 15 及以上，使用 ad-hoc 签名且未经 Apple 公证。

## Downloads and task management

- Click footer status counts, such as downloading, completed or failed, to filter the task list. Hover and selected states make these controls easier to find.
- Metadata extraction and downloads now have separate queues, each allowing up to three concurrent tasks. Waiting downloads can resolve titles, covers and formats in advance. Live captures retain their separate slot.
- Paused or interrupted downloads reuse saved extraction results and matching partial files. Rejected media URLs trigger at most one fresh extraction, while partial files from different sources remain separate.
- When media has finished downloading but subtitles or other attachments fail, retries can reuse the downloaded sources.

## Reliability and processing

- Temporary cookie and extraction files left by an abnormal exit are safely removed on the next startup after acquiring the data-directory lock. Saved sign-ins are preserved.
- Deleting an unfinished task while keeping media now removes its abandoned source and processing caches. Exported files are preserved, including protection for outputs placed inside older cache directories.
- Eligible SDR H.264/MKV cover intros reuse the main video stream to reduce full re-encoding, while retaining output and attachment-timing checks.
- Updated Vitest to address known vulnerabilities and corrected the filename-conflict acceptance test for saved metadata before transfer.

## Upgrade notes

- Fully quit the app, including its tray process, or stop the old container before upgrading. Keep your existing data and download directories.
- Older tasks without saved metadata, or tasks whose source URLs have changed, may still restart their transfer. Partial files from different sources are never combined blindly.
- This update does not automatically scan or delete orphaned caches whose task records were already removed by older versions.
- macOS still requires version 15 or later and uses ad-hoc signing without Apple notarization.
