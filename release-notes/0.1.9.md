# Huifa Media Downloader 0.1.9

## 中文

- 切换到已有下载目录时，可快速扫描一级目录并将未记录、非空的音视频文件导入为已完成历史；原始文件不会被移动、删除或重新下载。
- 本地导入的历史任务会明确保护原文件，禁用重新下载和格式转换等会改动来源文件的操作。
- 优化下载任务的状态汇总、任务卡交互和列表浏览体验。

## English

- When switching to an existing download folder, the app can quickly scan its top-level files and import unrecorded, non-empty media as completed history without moving, deleting, or redownloading the originals.
- Imported local-history tasks protect their source files by disabling operations that could modify them, such as redownload and format conversion.
- Improved download status summaries, task-card interaction, and task-list browsing.
